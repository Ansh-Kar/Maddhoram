Our Story

Three generations of mithai

Maddhoram began in 1985 as a tiny halwai shop in Old Town Bhubaneswar, run by our grandfather with two copper kadhais and a handwritten notebook of recipes. Three generations later, not much has changed — we still churn our own chenna, still fry jalebi in batches through the morning, and still slow-cook our dal makhani overnight.

The kitchen has grown, though. Alongside the mithai, our chefs now serve the Indian dishes families ask for — from masala dosas at breakfast to Hyderabadi biryani on Sunday afternoons. And our signature gift boxes carry the same promise as the first one our grandfather ever packed: traditional sweets, savoris, dry fruits and confectioneries, wrapped in deep crimson and gold.
